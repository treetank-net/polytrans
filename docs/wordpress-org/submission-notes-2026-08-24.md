> PolyTrans 1.20.0.
>
> PolyTrans is an integration built around Polylang – it reads languages and translation
> relationships from it. Compatibility only: not affiliated with or endorsed by the Polylang
> authors, and the slug is polytrans, not polylang-anything. Ours and opensource since July 2025.
> Repo: https://gitlab.com/treetank/polytrans; mirror: https://github.com/treetank-net/polytrans.
>
> No translation service is bundled – the site owner uses their own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google
> key; every endpoint is listed under "== External services ==" in readme. No telemetry. vendor/
> is Composer-installed twig/twig 3.28 (BSD-3-Clause).
>
> Additional services in plugin: workflows (allow posts to be modified by ai or reviewed), ai
> costs summary, external translation services integration (manual endpoint override).
>
